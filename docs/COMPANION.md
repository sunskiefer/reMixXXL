# Companion web page

reMixXXL serves a page for a phone or PC browser (works on the Force's own browser too).

- Address: `http://<IP of the Force>:6731/` (port 6731; if it is taken the next free port is used; the log line
  `web: companion page at ...` in `remixxxl_diag.log` shows the real address). Phone and Force must be on the same network.
- QR: on the WHEEL page tap the small black circle in the middle of the platter: the QR of that address replaces the BPM;
  tap again to hide it.
- Shows 1, 2, 3 or 4 decks as stacked waveform lanes; the deck buttons choose which are shown.
- Drag a lane to turn that deck's wheel: VINYL spins the track, CDJ nudges the tempo; each lane chooses pitch or spin.
- SELECT shows each lane's controls over the waveform. SELECT + ZOOM together hides those controls so a tap on the track
  sets where it plays from.
- ZOOM: pinch / mouse wheel; the scale buttons set seconds across.
- The small round dot next to ZOOM makes the page full screen.
